Let’s get rolling.

Roll the coral block across the board and drop it upright into the green goal.

Stay on solid ground. Both halves of the block need a tile beneath them. Roll off an edge and you can try again.

Go lightly on copper. Fragile tiles support a flat block, but break when you stand upright.

Open a new path. Stand upright on a round switch to toggle its dotted bridge tiles.
